## New funding and BEAM workflows

Government, company, NGO, donor and individual sponsor records have school-scoped codes and dynamic sponsor-receivable mappings. Awards retain year, source, reference, vote coverage, limits, dates, status, utilisation, remaining funding, settlement totals and audit history.

The Funding hub supports CSV template download, preview, exact admission/name matching, validation, duplicate detection, source-document/evidence references, classification, and independent approval. Batches are separated by sponsor and academic year. Historical descriptions alone do not create claims or cash. Unverified rows remain in the existing migration staging architecture. Classified bank deposits, historical allocations and adjustments remain staging evidence for a separately reviewed migration, rather than inferred receipts.

Claims retain approved, recognised, pre-cutover received, outstanding, disputed and rejected amounts, submission dates, currency, fee votes and evidence. Pending claims can be prepared for recognition and independently checked later. Approved financial history is corrected through checked reversals. A replacement claim records the superseded claim, releases its active duplicate identity, and cannot precede the correction date; existing opening GL is not posted again.

Settlement preparation records actual cash confirmation and one or more claim/application allocations, including multiple students and academic years. A different management checker approves posting. Duplicate references with identical terms return the original result; changed terms are rejected. Transactions lock school, target obligations and bank records and recheck outstanding balances. Controlled reversals retain original journals and generate compensating entries and cashbook offsets.

## Accounting matrix

All postings use the existing AccountingService and school-scoped dynamic mappings.

| Event | Debit | Credit | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original assessment | Student Debtors | Fee-vote revenue | Revenue once |
| Approved internal remission | Remission expense | Student Debtors | Provisional existing policy |
| Recognisable external/current claim | Sponsor receivable | Student Debtors | Transfers eligible unpaid votes |
| Existing sponsor opening | No new journal | No new journal | Links verified opening debit capacity |
| Existing student opening transferred | Sponsor receivable | Student Debtors | Reduces opening invoice and student control |
| Verified missing historical opening | Sponsor receivable | Explicit opening equity mapping | Reviewed correction; no 2026 revenue |
| Actual sponsor receipt | Bank | Sponsor receivable | Cashbook and allocations updated |
| Checked reversal | Exact original credits | Exact original debits | Original remains posted |

Unverified, pending, rejected and disputed unrecognised amounts create no assets or cash. Recognition requires documentary evidence and approved enforceability assessment. Existing opening links require an actual posted 1 January 2026 opening journal and sufficient unallocated debit capacity. Student reclassifications require an opening invoice and corresponding Student Debtors capacity. A previously linked opening amount cannot be counted in both controls. Releasing an opening subledger link leaves GL unchanged and exposes any unallocated control balance until a verified replacement is approved.
